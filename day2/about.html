About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight, distraction-free application built to capture your thoughts instantly. It helps you record and review notes without unnecessary complexity.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought into the "New note" text box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button.
  3. Review all your collected notes instantly.

Features

  • Lightning-fast note entry
  • Clean and accessible interface
  • No complex menus required

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
